# NEET-Health
Rising rates of young people Not in Education, Employment, or Training (NEET) explained by higher prevalence of physical and psychological ill health: a 15-year UK study


R-codes NEET part 1 are designed to combine Understanding Society waves a to n, code the necessary variables including specific health conditions and calculate the NEET relative prevalence ratios as well as condition specific ratios and relative prevalences. 

Stata codes (do.file) NEET part 2 is designed to produce Blinder-Oaxaca decomposition by year periods (2009-13, 2014-18, 2019-23), comparing each year to a reference period (2016), calculating within-person associations with a 1-year lag and produce stratified analyses. 

A preliminary version of the study is published on medRxiv: https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.08.11.26360217v1
